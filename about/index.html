About Great Multitude

Every church deserves to be found. Google will even pay for it.

Great Multitude is an ad agency for churches and nonprofits. We help qualifying organizations claim up to $10,000 a month in free Google Search ads, then build and run every campaign, so the people searching for them find them first.

Why churches

Somebody in your city is looking for a church this week.

They don't drive around reading signs. They pick up their phone and search: “church near me,” “Christmas Eve service,” “grief support group.” Whoever shows up first gets the visit. Too often, that isn't the church down the street.

  1. 01 · Stewardship

    Free means not one dollar from the offering.

    Every dollar a church spends on ads is a dollar it doesn't spend on ministry. The Ad Grant takes that trade off the table. Google covers the clicks, so your giving stays with your mission.

  2. 02 · Access

    Free puts every church on the same page.

    Big churches have marketing teams and budgets. The church of 120 on Main Street usually doesn't. The grant can put both at the top of the same search results. We make sure yours is ready to be there.

  3. 03 · Urgency

    Free and unused is the same as nothing.

    The grant is a monthly allowance, not a savings account. Whatever a qualifying church doesn't use this month is gone. Many churches never apply. Others lose the grant because the rules are strict and nobody on staff has time to keep up. That's the gap we close.

Why we do this

For a business, a good ad means a sale. For a church, it means a person.

We've run paid campaigns for businesses on Google, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn, where every click costs real money and every result gets measured.

Churches deserve that same discipline. The difference is that with the Ad Grant, Google covers the clicks.

And the results look different. A family walks in on Sunday for the first time. A teenager finds a youth group. Someone grieving finds a room full of people who care. That's what we're after, and it's why church work sits at the center of what we do.

So every campaign gets judged by one question: is it bringing people through the door? If it isn't, we change it.

We count real people, not clicks.
