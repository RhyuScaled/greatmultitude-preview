Why churches

Somebody in your city is looking for a church this week.

They don't drive around reading signs. They pick up their phone and search: “church near me,” “Christmas Eve service,” “grief support group.” Whoever shows up first gets the visit.

  1. 01 · Stewardship

    Free means not one dollar from the offering.

    Every dollar a church spends on ads is a dollar it doesn't spend on ministry. The Ad Grant takes that trade off the table. Google covers the clicks, so your giving stays with your mission.

  2. 02 · Access

    Free puts every church on the same page.

    Big churches have marketing teams and budgets. The church of 120 on Main Street usually doesn't. The grant can put both at the top of the same search results. We make sure yours is ready to be there.

  3. 03 · Urgency

    Free and unused is the same as nothing.

    The grant is a monthly allowance, not a savings account. Whatever a qualifying church doesn't use this month is gone. Many churches never apply. Others lose the grant because the rules are strict and nobody on staff has time to keep up. That's the gap we close.

$10,000 is the ceiling, not the starting line.

How much of the grant you use depends on how many people search in your area. A small-town church uses less than one in a big city. That's normal.

Every campaign you add opens up a new set of searches. That's why churches running more ministries usually use more of the grant.

The catch

The ads are free. Running them isn't.

Google sets strict rules for grant accounts and checks them every month. Miss them and the account gets suspended.

  1. 01

    CTR ≥ 5.0% · monthly

    Keep a 5% click-through rate

    At least 5 of every 100 people who see your ads have to click. Every month.

  2. 02

    Conversion tracking required

    Track real results

    Visit sign-ups, calls and other key actions have to be counted correctly inside Google Ads.

  3. 03

    No single-word keywords

    Follow the keyword rules

    No single-word or overly broad keywords. “Church” alone won't fly. “Church near me” will.

  4. 04

    2+ ad groups · 2+ ads · sitelinks

    Build it Google's way

    Every campaign needs the required ad groups, ads and sitelinks before it can run.

That's our job. You lead the church. We keep the grant compliant and your ads running every single week.
